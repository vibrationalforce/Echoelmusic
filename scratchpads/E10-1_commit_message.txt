feat(record): E10-1 — an armed audio track records the microphone

Founder 2026-10-04: "Audio-Eingang soll Aufnahme, Sampling und Analyse
ermöglichen; Mikrofonzugriff wird ausdrücklich aktiviert." This is the
smallest complete path, recording only.

The arm switch on an audio track asks for microphone permission (the one
MicAccess.request() call site). Record starts MicTakeRecorder, an
AVAudioRecorder that sits OUTSIDE the playback graph and writes to
Media/Audio. Stop, or reaching the end of the piece, commits the take as
a part on that track through the existing take commit. One track records
at a time and there is no monitoring (use headphones).

Why it is shaped this way:
- The #1302 crash path stays forbidden. Nothing here touches .inputNode
  or installs an input tap in the engine graph. Claim 6 pins this, and
  the only two installTap sites tap the app's own output.
- The route owner RecordRouteOwner.audioTrack is claimed in arm() before
  the recorder starts, because a category change restarts a running
  engine. It is released on every exit (#299). Claim 2 pins this; the
  old "no claimant" guard is inverted into
  TheRecordRouteHasOneClaimantTests, not deleted.
- NSMicrophoneUsageDescription comes back in the same commit as the owner
  case. iOS terminates an app that asks without the key.
  EveryPermissionPromptHasACapabilityTests pins the pairing.
- A failure is shown at the Record button (audioTakeProblem), not only
  logged.

Claims stay below what ships. The site, CLAIMS.md and privacy pages say
recording arrives with the next version and is being tested. Nothing
is claimed outward until G9 (FOUNDER_INBOX) is confirmed on a device.
