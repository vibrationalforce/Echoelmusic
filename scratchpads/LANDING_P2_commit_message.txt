  RTMPHaishinKit) and Logboard. HaishinKit is pinned to the COMMIT behind
  tag 2.2.5, dc880cb540b8feeb98f64e8b7dcfaaf320b6b2bd: a tag can be

  the master mix after the final chain (the ring RetroCapture reads) and
  the rendered visual go through bounded AsyncStreams into RTMPStream on
  one host-clock T0. The realtime audio callback is never touched. No
  camera (rPPG keeps it), no microphone (E10-1 keeps the record route).
- Logboard's console appender is replaced by a silent one before any
  connection exists: HaishinKit logs unhandled server answers at .info,
  and an onFCPublish answer repeats the publish name — the stream key.
