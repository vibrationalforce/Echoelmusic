# Future Management — structured foresight for emerging technologies

**Principle:** Every future technology is scored against the homogeneous Echoel vision before any planning or public narrative. Hardware and sensors attach as OSC/MIDI/Art-Net subscribers. Science-only. Inclusion = access. Sustainability and environmental protection are first-class. Respect and commercial success come from honest, high-quality work.

## When to activate (Claude loads this willingly)

- Mentions of Quantum Computing, Mobility, Digitalization, Internet of Things, Neuralink, BCI, traffic stress, maritime, air/space travel, or any "future tech" / horizon scan.
- Strategic planning that looks beyond the current Store-first DMMW.
- Ideas that could expand Tauchfliege, vibration installations, or bio-reactive control into new domains.

## Alignment Filter (mandatory)

1. **Vision pillars:** Does it advance Umweltschutz, Frieden (transparency/open standards), Inklusion (access), or sustainability?
2. **Red lines:** No health/therapy claims. Mechanism only. Open standards preferred. No lock-in. Hardware = subscriber.
3. **Homogeneous architecture:** Can it attach to the existing bus (OSC etc.) without a new codebase?
4. **Tier:** LIVE / ROADMAP / WATCH / NORTH STAR / REJECT. Default for speculative tech is WATCH.
5. **Wealth + regard:** Prefer paths that ship high-quality product today while documenting honest future research.

## Procedure

1. Ground in `memory/vision.md`, `vision-alignment` skill, and FOUNDER_PRODUCT_LAW.
2. Produce a compact Technology Card (see template below).
3. Suggest the smallest reversible next step (research note, OSC interface sketch, or "log and park").
4. Log material decisions to `inspiration.csv` or `decisions.csv`.

## Technology Card Template

```
### [Technology Name]
Tier: WATCH / ROADMAP / NORTH STAR
Alignment: [Env / Peace / Inclusion / Sustainability] score + one-sentence rationale
Risks: [overclaim, energy, ethics, lock-in]
Echoel link: [how it attaches as subscriber or extends Tauchfliege/installation]
Next step: [smallest reversible action]
```

## Domain Starting Points (expand in domain files or cards)

- **Quantum Computing:** WATCH. Potential future acceleration of DSP, fluid simulation for amphibious, or optimization. No near-term claim.
- **Mobility / Amphibious:** ROADMAP. Tauchfliege core. Low-impact hybrid air-water, marine sensing.
- **Digitalization & IoT:** ROADMAP. Expand open sensor networks for env monitoring, citizen science.
- **Neuralink / BCI:** WATCH. Extreme bio-signal research. Strict science-only, optional modulation only.
- **Traffic stress reduction:** WATCH. Haptic or bio-reactive calm systems. Mechanism, not effect.
- **Maritime / Air / Space:** NORTH STAR. Extension of amphibious platform + open control into extreme environments. Sustainability focus.

All domains stay pipeline-only until a concrete, founder-approved, red-line-compliant prototype exists.
