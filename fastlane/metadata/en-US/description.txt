Echoel is a music workstation for iPhone and iPad: instruments, effects, a piano roll and a drum grid, an arrangement timeline, a session grid for live sets, audio and MIDI recording, mixing, automation and export. Your songs are files on your device in an open, documented format, and the app needs no account.


TRACKS AND SOUND

- Songs hold instrument, audio and return tracks (up to 32, 64 and 8). Add any kind, or add a drum track that comes with an empty one-bar pattern to fill. Recolor, mute, solo and delete tracks.
- The built-in 16-voice synth with six engines (Classic, Supersaw, FM, Sub, Morph and Mallet), one Character control per engine, a Clean or Ladder filter, presets and adjustable sound parameters.
- A synthesized eight-piece drum kit (kick, snare, clap, two toms, closed and open hi-hat, crash) on General MIDI drum note numbers.
- A sampler that plays an imported audio file (mixed to mono, up to ten minutes) across the keyboard, at its original pitch on C4.
- Installed third-party Audio Unit instruments (stereo), with their own interface on the track's Sound page; their settings are saved with the song.
- An insert chain of up to eight effects on every track and the master: Echoel EQ, Compressor, Delay, Reverb, Chorus, Phaser and Flanger, or installed stereo Audio Unit effects.
- The seven Echoel effects are also available as Audio Units for other apps.


PLAY, WRITE AND ARRANGE

- Play any instrument track from the on-screen keys, or live from a MIDI keyboard or controller by USB, Bluetooth or network MIDI. The synth and plugin instruments follow pitch bend, the mod wheel and the sustain pedal; the drum kit and sampler play notes.
- A piano roll for synth, sampler and plugin tracks: tap to add or remove a note, drag to move it or change its length, on a grid from 1/4 to 1/32 or 1/8 and 1/16 triplets.
- A step grid for drum tracks, with one row for each kit piece. Tap a step to set or clear a hit, hold it for an accent.
- Arrange clips on a bar timeline: drag them in whole bars, duplicate them or take them off the timeline. Tap an audio track to import a file at a bar, then move it and trim either edge to the beat.
- A session grid launches clips and scenes on the next bar, the next quarter note or at once, stops a track or all session clips, and hands every track back to the arrangement.


MIDI

- Record a MIDI performance onto an armed instrument track. Each take becomes a new clip with its notes, pitch bend and controllers, as one undo step.
- MIDI Learn maps a knob or fader to a mixer control, a synth parameter or a built-in effect parameter.
- Send MIDI clock to the outputs you choose, or follow an external MIDI clock.
- Novation Launchpad Mini MK3, X and Pro MK3 become a window onto the session grid.


RECORD, MIX, EXPORT

- Record mono audio from the microphone or one interface input onto an armed audio track. Takes stay on the device.
- A channel strip for every track and the master, with fader, pan and a peak meter; sends to up to eight return tracks; any track can go to its own output pair of a multichannel interface.
- Draw automation for volume, pan, sends, synth and plugin parameters, or record it by moving the control while the song plays.
- A Bluetooth heart-rate chest strap can be a modulation source: route heart rate, HRV or coherence to a fader, pan, send or synth parameter, with depth and inversion per route. With no strap, the control stays where you set it, and an export never reads the strap.
- Export the arrangement as a 24-bit, 48 kHz stereo WAV with its loudness (LUFS) and true peak reported, as stems (one WAV per track, before the master effects), or as a Standard MIDI file (.mid).
- Songs save automatically as .echoel files, an open and documented JSON format. The Files app shows them, and a song sent by AirDrop opens in Echoel.


No account, no ads, no tracking, no server of ours.
