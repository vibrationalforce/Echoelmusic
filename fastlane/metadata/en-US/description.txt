Your body plays it. Heart rate, HRV and breath drive a synthesizer, a live visual and your light rig in real time — on iPhone, on-device, no account.
Finger on the camera, pulse locks, and the music moves with you. Touch the visual and every finger lands in key.
Then send it on: OSC, MIDI and MPE out, Art-Net and sACN light, ADM-OSC spatial objects. Echoel sits inside the rig you already have.


THE BODY AS CONTROLLER

Read your pulse from Apple Health (e.g. Apple Watch), a Bluetooth heart-rate strap, or the iPhone camera (rPPG):

- Heart rate shapes tempo and vibrato
- HRV and coherence shape brightness, harmonicity and texture
- Breath shapes the amplitude envelope — the sound swells and settles once with each breath
- The picture is playable: touch the visual and your fingers become notes, quantized to the take's key and scale

Not every source measures everything: camera = pulse, HRV, breath; chest strap = pulse, HRV; Apple Watch = heart rate via Health. What isn't measured doesn't modulate.

No two takes are the same.


CREATE

- Bio-reactive additive synthesis engine (DDSP) — vDSP-accelerated, no external dependencies
- Design and save your own patches
- Your voice becomes the instrument's timbre: hold a tone and Echoel measures its spectral envelope — about 64 numbers, no audio recorded — a saved patch can keep it
- Composition: pick a genre and a key; your body generates in-key material you shape with the sound and character controls
- Concert pitch (A4 / Kammerton) set exactly to the cent; lock a BPM for tight loops or stay sync-free
- Effects: reverb, delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, tremolo, saturation, harmonizer and more
- Immersive visual: the notes you play become color in real time — each tone's frequency transposed by whole octaves into visible light — reacting to the music and your body
- Record the visual as it plays: an MP4 in your photo library with the music on it (at most the last 30 s of sound)
- Retroactive capture: keep the loop you just played
- Export the loop as audio (WAV) or MIDI (.mid) for your DAW — the MIDI file carries tempo, 4/4 and the key signature, in whole bars


CONNECT

- MIDI note input and output (output enabled in the routing panel)
- MPE output to your rig — the zone is announced, notes spread over member channels 2-16, each carrying glide, slide and pressure (two switches in the routing panel, both off by default; the input side stays plain MIDI notes)
- OSC output for live rigs and visuals
- ADM-OSC immersive object output — on a connected spatial rig this is where your body moves the sound in space: breath sweeps it left and right, calm lifts it, coherence pulls it close
- Art-Net native light output for installations and stage
- sACN (E1.31) light output — unicast to an address you set; multicast is not available on iOS
- External display: the visual plays on a connected screen or projector (USB-C/HDMI or AirPlay) as its own stage; the phone stays the instrument


PRIVACY

Your biometrics stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and the app talks to no server of ours.

Bio values leave the device only when you switch an output on, and only on your local network: OSC, ADM-OSC, Art-Net or sACN light (heart rate, HRV, breath and coherence become DMX values), or a nearby shared session. Optional: write heart rate and respiratory rate to Apple Health; use your location for the place in a session name or local weather (Apple Weather) shaping the music — both off by default, coordinates never stored. What you create is yours.


ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility-first: VoiceOver, large legible bio readouts, exact numeric entry for every numeric parameter, and a 3 Hz visual flash cap for photosensitive safety.
