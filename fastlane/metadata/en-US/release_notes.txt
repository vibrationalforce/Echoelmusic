Echoel 11 is a new app, rebuilt as a music workstation for iPhone and iPad.

- Six synth engines, a drum kit and a sampler; installed Audio Unit instruments and effects on tracks
- Piano roll, drum step grid, arrangement timeline and a session grid for live sets
- On-screen keys, MIDI keyboards and controllers, MIDI Learn, MIDI clock in and out
- Audio recording from the microphone or an interface; MIDI recording as clips
- Mixer with sends and return tracks, automation, and a Bluetooth heart-rate strap as a modulation source
- Export as 24-bit WAV, stems or a Standard MIDI file (.mid); songs are open .echoel files

Songs from earlier versions of Echoel are not imported by this version.

Echoel is in active development. Feedback welcome: echoel@tropicaldrones.com
