How it works

The cycle is tied to Coordinated Universal Time: ten seconds long, four in, six out, starting on every tenth second. Your device sets its clock from the network, usually to within a few hundredths of a second — far closer than anyone can feel across a ten-second breath. Open it in Hamburg, Lagos or Lima: same moment, same breath.

Nothing runs behind it. Once the page has loaded it keeps going without a connection, because the only thing it reads is your own clock.

Alle, die diese Seite offen haben, atmen im selben Moment ein und aus — überall auf der Erde. Vier Sekunden ein, sechs Sekunden aus, nach der Weltzeit. Kein Server, kein Konto, keine Daten.

What it is not

It is not a beat and not a tempo. Music over the internet cannot be held to a shared beat without a dedicated clock, and this page does not pretend otherwise. One slow cycle is what a plain clock can carry honestly.

It is a shared moment, not a programme. Breathe only as feels comfortable, and stop if you feel light-headed.

Nothing leaves your device

Nothing is sent, nothing is stored, nobody is counted. The optional sound is made on your device and follows the cycle; it starts only when you ask for it.
