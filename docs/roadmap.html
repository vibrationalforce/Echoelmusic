Roadmap

Where Echoel is today, what comes next, and in which order. The dates are plans, not promises: when something is not ready, it moves, and the order stays. As of 19 October 2026.

In TestFlight now: Echoel Studio

Echoel Studio is a music workstation for iPhone and iPad. It is a fresh start, built since October 2026, and it is still being tested on devices. This build holds:

  • Instruments: a synthesizer, drums and a sampler, played from a MIDI keyboard, or written as notes and drum steps.
  • Effects: EQ, compressor, delay and reverb, on tracks and on the master.
  • Arrangement and automation: parts on tracks, parameter automation, undo.
  • A session view for live sets: scenes you launch on the beat or the bar.
  • Recording: MIDI takes with pitch bend and controllers, and audio takes on an audio track. Echoel asks for the microphone only when you arm an audio track or set one to hear its input; a recording is saved in your song on the device.
  • Mixing and export: meters on every track, a master chain, WAV export with loudness and true-peak measurement, and MIDI file export.
  • Echoel's own instruments for other apps: the synthesizer, drums and sampler as Audio Units. They are not yet tested in a host app on a device, and they are not on sale yet.
  • An assistant on the device: you describe a change to the song in words, it shows what it did, and one undo takes it back. It uses the language model on the device, where the device has one, and needs no network.
  • The network: this build contacts the App Store when you open the store screen, and uses the local network only when you turn on Network MIDI, which is off until you do.

The earlier app

Until October 2026, TestFlight carried the earlier Echoel app: an instrument played by the body, with a camera pulse reading, Bluetooth heart-rate straps, generative visuals and OSC, Art-Net and sACN output. It is frozen at version 10.79.515. Its parts that worked move into Echoel Studio as separate modules, in the order below. Most other pages of this site still describe that earlier app.

What stays the same

  • The app is free to download. No account, no ads, no tracking, no server of ours.
  • No subscription. Echoel's own instruments for other apps are bought once.
  • The source code is public under the MIT licence, and songs are files in an open, documented format.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you ask for it, such as opening the store or turning on a connection, and nothing goes to a server of ours.
  • Before 1.0, every screen is checked with VoiceOver, with larger text and with reduced motion.
  • Echoel is a tool for making music. It is not a medical product and makes no health promise.
