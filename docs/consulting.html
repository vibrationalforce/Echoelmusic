I’m Echoel (Michael Terbuyken), a music producer, sound artist and developer in Hamburg. Since 2006 I have been creating interactive installations, video, visuals, light and musical performance. Under Echoelmusic I offer the applied, technical side of this work: the app Echoelmusic, and consulting for your projects.

How I work

I consult, plan and support online: by call, by message and in shared documents. Work on site, such as measuring and tuning a system or setting up, is done by your crew or by local partners, following my plan.

What to send

A few lines are enough for a first answer:

  • What the project is, in two or three sentences.
  • The date or deadline.
  • What already exists: room, system, material, team.
  • What you are after: a call, a written plan, project support or a workshop.

You receive the cost framework with the planning.
