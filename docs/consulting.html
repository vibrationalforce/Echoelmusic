Consulting · Planning · Delivery

Sound, space and immersive formats — from the first question to the finished project.

I’m Echoel (Michael Terbuyken), a music producer, sound artist and developer in Hamburg. Since 2006 I have been creating interactive installations, video, visuals, light and musical performance. Under Echoelmusic I offer the applied, technical side of this work: the app Echoelmusic, and consulting for your projects.

Three areas

Sound & Content

Sound for brands · Multimedia production · Content for social media

Space & Acoustics

Sound reinforcement and acoustics for clubs, open air, trade fairs and studios.

Immersive & Inclusive

Multidimensional and immersive formats · Making music perceptible for everyone: installations with vibration, water, light and sound.

Further services around music production, consulting, and artistic and conceptual work on request.

How it works

The same three stages in all three areas:

  1. Consulting: a call. We clarify the goal, the venue, the audience and the scope.
  2. Planning: concept, plan and cost framework, in writing.
  3. Delivery: project support through to completion.

How I work

I consult, plan and support remotely. Work on site — at trade fairs, measuring and tuning a system, and setting up — is done by local partners following my plan.

Online workshops

Workshops take place online, among them “AI in media production”: how I produce software and media with AI agents — traceable in Echoelmusic’s public repository.

The tool: Echoelmusic

Echoelmusic is my music workstation for iPhone and iPad. It is in TestFlight and still being tested on devices. More about Echoelmusic
