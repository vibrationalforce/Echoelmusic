Echoel

Music · Performance · Immersive and multi-sensory installation

Sound you can stand inside: electronic music, live performance and walk-in installations where sound becomes water, light and vibration. Michael Terbuyken, Hamburg.

Also: audio development · production · consulting

Since 2006 Echoel has made electronic music, played it live and built walk-in installations in which vibrating water, light and sound let one piece of music be heard, seen and felt at once — most recently a vibration pontoon and sound mirror workshop at Burgbeben STRNBRG 2024. Echoel makes music with the Tropical Drones collective and built the music app Echoelmusic.
