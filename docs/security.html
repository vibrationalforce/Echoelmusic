Security

Updated: November 2026

Privacy-First Design
Local-first, and no servers. Echoelmusic stores and processes your data on your device, protected by Apple's on-device Data Protection. No accounts, no tracking, nothing sent to us. Anything that leaves the device does so only over a connection you switch on yourself; for Echoelmusic, the app in TestFlight, the full list is in its section of the Privacy Policy. In the earlier app (10.79), biosignal values left the device only over OSC/ADM-OSC, lighting output or nearby session sharing, and Apple Health data was excluded from those; the first two are plain UDP and unencrypted by design, while nearby sharing is encrypted device-to-device.

Local-First Architecture

Echoelmusic processes all data locally on your device. We do not operate external servers or cloud backends for user data. This means:

  • No user accounts: No registration, no passwords to leak
  • No data collection: we never collect or receive your personal data — nothing is sent to us
  • No analytics tracking: No third-party analytics or ad SDKs
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs: the instrument is built on Apple native frameworks

Encryption & Data at Rest

  • Apple Data Protection: On-device files are encrypted at rest by iOS Data Protection (hardware-backed), unlocked only while the device is.
  • No servers: we operate none, so your data is never sent to us and there is no account or database to breach.
  • What you switch on does travel: if you explicitly enable OSC / ADM-OSC output or Art-Net / sACN lighting, biosignal values leave the device unencrypted to a destination you choose — plain UDP, because those open standards define no encryption, and that is the point of those features. Treat that network as you would any stage or studio LAN. Nearby session sharing is different: it runs device-to-device over Apple's MultipeerConnectivity with encryption required, so it is not on that list. In all cases, values read from Apple Health or Apple Watch are excluded and never leave. In Echoelmusic, body values go out on the Connections page only while you allow it there, and a live stream started with Go Live sends the Field’s picture and the master mix — no body measurement — to the streaming service you entered.

No Accounts, No Login

  • Nothing to sign into: Echoelmusic has no user accounts, no passwords, and stores no credentials — so there is nothing to authenticate or leak.
  • You hold the keys: Access to the camera, the microphone, Bluetooth, the local network and your location — and, in the earlier app, HealthKit — is gated entirely by the iOS permission prompts you grant, and can be revoked any time in Settings. Echoelmusic asks for the microphone only when you arm an audio track or set one to hear its input; the earlier app (10.79) does not use it.

Biometric Data Handling

In the earlier app, heart rate, HRV and other biometric data read from Apple HealthKit is:

  • Processed locally: All biometric-to-audio mapping happens on-device in real time
  • Never transmitted: Biometric data is not sent to any server or third party
  • Not stored permanently: Session data exists only during active use
  • User-controlled: Biometric access requires explicit permission and can be revoked at any time

In Echoelmusic, heart rate, HRV and coherence derived from a Bluetooth chest strap stay on the device and are not written to a file; a song stores only which source moves which value. They leave the device only if you allow body sharing on the Connections page. Apple Health is not in Echoelmusic.

Echoelmusic is not a medical device. Biometric readings are used for creative and entertainment purposes only. See our Health Disclaimer.

Privacy by Design

  • Minimal data, by design: The app collects no personal data, has no analytics, and shares nothing with third parties — the practical foundation of GDPR and CCPA is achieved by collecting essentially nothing.
  • Nothing sold or shared: No personal information is ever sold, rented, or transmitted off your device.
  • Local processing: Data minimization and on-device processing are core architectural principles, not afterthoughts.

For full details on data handling, see our Privacy Policy.

Open Source & Transparency

The earlier app is built on native Apple frameworks only (Core Audio, HealthKit, Metal, AVFoundation, CoreMIDI). Echoelmusic adds one open-source library, HaishinKit (BSD-3), for its live stream. Neither includes analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

We believe transparency builds trust. If you have questions about our security practices, we are happy to discuss them.

Limitations

No software is perfectly secure. While we follow security best practices, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Specifically:

  • Security depends on your device's OS version and security updates
  • Once you switch an output on, we cannot protect what you sent — not the transport (see above: the local-network outputs are plain UDP by design), and not the receiving end. A lighting desk, a TouchDesigner patch or the other person's phone may log, forward or display your values; that is their software, not ours
  • Biometric sensor accuracy depends on hardware and conditions outside our control
  • We encourage you to keep your device updated and use strong device passcodes
