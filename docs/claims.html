What we do not claim

Most software tells you what it does. This page tells you what Echoelmusic deliberately does not do — thirteen entries, each with the reason. It is the public half of a register we keep internally and check on every build.

Why a page like this exists

Echoelmusic is built by one person, in the open, and it changes fast. Twice we shipped copy describing a feature that had been removed — once on this website, once in an App Store listing. Both took a full cycle to undo. So we started writing down what is not true, next to why, and we check the website against that list automatically. If a sentence anywhere contradicts this page, this page is the one that is right.

Where it runs

It is an Audio Unit — but we only claim the host we tested

Echoelmusic is an AUv3 instrument, and it has been confirmed loading and sounding in AUM on a device. We do not claim Logic Pro, GarageBand or Ableton Live until each one has been run and measured. One host is one host. And in the other direction: Echoelmusic never hosts other people’s plugins — that was removed on purpose and is not coming back.

The Apple Watch does not measure for it, and does not control it

A watch app exists as a display. It reads what the iPhone writes; it does not send a pulse back, and the transport for that direction is not built. Even if it were, watch heart rate arrives with roughly four to five seconds of latency — usable for a trend, never for beat sync. The real sensor is your finger on the phone camera.

It is not in the App Store yet

Today there is TestFlight only. Any “download on the App Store” badge or store link you see elsewhere is premature, and we will change this line on the day the first release is approved — not before.

What it is not

Not wellness, meditation, sleep, focus, stress relief, longevity or “HealthTech”

Biofeedback here is a control signal, the way a keyboard or a breath controller is. We describe the mechanism — a calmer pulse makes different music — and never an effect on you. We do not claim it calms you, helps you sleep, or improves any health marker. The app itself says its data is for self-observation, not medical diagnosis, and the marketing is not allowed to say something the app contradicts.

Not “biohacking”

Same reason as above, and it gets its own line because it once slipped past that one. It is the word that files this instrument under self-optimisation culture, which is exactly where it does not belong. It appears in no script, caption, hashtag or title.

It does not post anything anywhere

Echoelmusic has no social-media integration. It does not publish to TikTok, YouTube or Instagram, automatically or otherwise, and there is no AI agent or tool protocol running inside the app. What you export, you export yourself.

Features that are gone, or were never there

No drums, sampler, video editing, livestream or multitrack recorder

All of these were either removed deliberately or never wired up. There is no drum machine, no sample import, no video capture or editing, no livestreaming (RTMP was scoped and cut — never built), and no multitrack audio recorder you can reach. Echoelmusic is an instrument, not a workstation, and that boundary was a decision rather than a shortfall.

No microphone: no voice recording, cloning, AI voice, text-to-speech or autotune

The audio input path was removed entirely. Nothing in the app listens. There is no pitch correction on your voice, no harmoniser, no vocoder, no voice model of any kind. What the app records is its own output.

No subscription, no paywall, nothing to buy

Version 1.0 is free and complete. There is no “Pro” tier you can purchase today, no monthly or annual access, and no recurring revenue to report. Later versions may introduce paid tiers; when they do, this line changes first.

Precision about what the body actually drives

Breath depth and LF/HF are measured, but they do not shape the sound

Both are pinned to a neutral value everywhere they reach the synthesis engine, so neither currently changes what you hear — and both sound impressive in a caption, which is precisely why they are listed here. What is real: coherence, heart rate, heart-rate variability and breath phase each drive audible parameters, and the coherence trend morphs the timbre as it rises or falls.

MPE goes out, not in

Echoelmusic can send MPE to your rig, as a switch you turn on. It does not receive MPE: the input parses the traffic but does not distinguish zones, so per-note expression arriving from a controller is not separated per note. Pitch bend, pressure and slide all sound — on one voice.

An external controller plays the body voice — singular

Plug in a MIDI controller and it sounds immediately, ahead of the breath envelope. But exactly one voice consumes those events and it is monophonic. We do not say “plays the voices”, “play chords” or “polyphonic” about that path.

What it is built on

Zero external dependencies — so also zero of the usual suspects

Echoelmusic ships with no third-party packages at all. That means no Composable Architecture, no RevenueCat, no SwiftData, no VideoToolbox and no HaishinKit — several of which have been attributed to it. It is Swift, SwiftUI, and Apple frameworks, plus open standards on the wire: OSC, ADM-OSC, Art-Net, sACN and MIDI.
