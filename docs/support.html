Using the build

When does Echoelmusic ask for the microphone?

Only when you arm an audio track to record, or set one to hear its input. Echoelmusic records mono audio from the microphone or one interface input onto the armed track, and the takes stay on the device. If you said no, allow the microphone for Echoelmusic in the Settings app.

How do I use a Bluetooth heart-rate strap?

Put the strap on, then in the Song menu choose Body Source and Heart Rate Strap. Only then does iOS ask for Bluetooth. You do not pair it in the Settings app: Echoelmusic finds a strap that speaks the standard heart-rate service. The strap moves only what you route it to, and an export never reads it.

How do I export, and where are my songs?

Export the arrangement as a 24-bit, 48 kHz stereo WAV with its loudness (LUFS) and true peak reported, as stems (one WAV per track, before the master effects), or as a Standard MIDI File (.mid). Songs save automatically as .echoel files that the Files app shows, and a song sent by AirDrop opens in Echoelmusic.

Do Echoelmusic’s Audio Units work in other apps?

Echoelmusic’s instruments and effects are also Audio Units, but they have not yet been tested in a host app on a device, and the instruments are not on sale yet. If you try one, a line about what you saw helps.
