Echoel Studio

When does Echoel ask for the microphone?

Only when you arm an audio track to record, or set one to hear its input. Echoel records mono audio from the microphone or one interface input onto the armed track, and the takes stay on the device. If you said no, allow the microphone for Echoel in the Settings app.

How do I use a Bluetooth heart-rate strap?

Put the strap on, then in the Song menu choose Body Source and Heart Rate Strap. Only then does iOS ask for Bluetooth. You do not pair it in the Settings app: Echoel finds a strap that speaks the standard heart-rate service. The strap moves only what you route it to, and an export never reads it.

Do Echoel’s Audio Units work in other apps?

Echoel’s instruments and effects are also Audio Units, but they have not yet been tested in a host app on a device, and they are not on sale yet. Tell us what you see if you try one.

How do I export, and where are my songs?

Export the arrangement as a 24-bit, 48 kHz stereo WAV with its loudness (LUFS) and true peak reported, as stems (one WAV per track, before the master effects), or as a Standard MIDI File. Songs save automatically as .echoel files that the Files app shows, and a song sent by AirDrop opens in Echoel. The format is open and documented.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Song menu, choose Echoel Plugins and tap Restore Purchases. Without a network, Echoel reads the purchase record already on the device.

Apple Watch data not appearing?

Watch data arrives via Apple Health with a few seconds’ delay. Go to Settings > Privacy > Health and grant permission to Echoelmusic. For live readings use the camera pulse or a Bluetooth strap.

Audio latency issues?

Open the Master panel and set Audio latency to Low or Ultra. The tiers are buffer sizes — Normal is about 10.7 ms, Low about 5.3 ms, Ultra about 2.7 ms at 48 kHz — so a smaller one responds sooner and costs more CPU. iOS may refuse a tier, hardest over Bluetooth, and the row then shows the one it granted. The Audio timing line just below reports late blocks, so you can see whether a tier holds on your device.

Biofeedback not responding?

Check HealthKit permissions in Settings. Health-sourced data (Watch, ring) is delayed by design — the camera pulse and BLE straps are the live sources.

How do I export my creations?

In the earlier app, the Share button in any session exported a .wav render, a Standard MIDI File (.mid) or the project itself to Files, iCloud or other apps; it had no video export, because recording the visual was removed. Echoel Studio exports a WAV, stems or a MIDI file — see the FAQ.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All biometric data stays on your device. We don't collect or store any personal data. See our Privacy Policy.
