What is in the build

Version 11.0.0. It is new, and no part of it has been confirmed on a device yet; that is what the test is for.

Instruments and sound

A 16-voice synth with eleven engines: Classic, Supersaw, FM, Sub, Morph, Mallet, DDSP, Choir, Pluck, Wavetable and Texture. A synthesized eight-piece drum kit. A sampler that plays an imported audio file across the keyboard. Twenty-five built-in effects, up to eight on every track and on the master. Installed stereo Audio Units from other apps play on tracks.

Write and arrange

A piano roll and a drum step grid. An arrangement timeline with clips on bars. A session grid that launches clips and scenes on the next bar, the next quarter note or at once. Play from the on-screen keys or from a MIDI keyboard by USB, Bluetooth or network MIDI.

Record and mix

Record MIDI takes as clips, and mono audio from the microphone or one interface input; takes stay on the device. A channel strip with fader, pan and a peak meter on every track and the master, sends to return tracks, and automation that you draw or record.

Export

A 24-bit, 48 kHz stereo WAV with its loudness and true peak reported. Stems, one WAV per track. A Standard MIDI File. Import a MIDI file into the song.

Your songs are files

Songs save automatically as .echoel files that the Files app shows, in an open, documented format. The app needs no account. It has no ads, no tracking and no server of ours. What it reads, and what leaves the device only when you switch it on, is on the Privacy page.

Sources and outputs

Also in the build. Nothing is read or sent until you turn it on.

  • Heart-rate strap. A Bluetooth chest strap can be a modulation source: heart rate, HRV or coherence on a fader, pan, send or synth parameter. Without a strap the control stays where you set it.
  • Connections. OSC, ADM-OSC, Art-Net and sACN out to your local network. Body values are sent only while you allow body sharing on that page.
  • Go Live. Stream the Field’s picture and the master mix over RTMP or RTMPS to a service you choose. Nothing goes out until you press Go Live.
  • MPE out. The live track’s notes go to the MIDI outputs you pick, one channel per note.
  • Video tracks. Import a video from the photo library onto the timeline.

Who makes it

Echoelmusic is built in Hamburg by Echoel (Michael Terbuyken), a music producer, sound artist and developer. He also offers online consulting for sound and media production. More about him is on the artist page.

Details of the build and what comes next are on the Roadmap; questions are answered in the FAQ. The earlier body-driven app (10.79) is frozen; its documentation is archived.
