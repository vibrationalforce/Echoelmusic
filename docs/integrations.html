Integrations — what Echoel sends, and how to receive it

Echoel is the body-driven source at the front of your pipeline. Your pulse, breath and the music they compose leave the phone over open standards only — OSC, ADM-OSC, MIDI, Art-Net and sACN — so Reaper, TouchDesigner, Resolume, a lighting desk or a spatial renderer can follow a performer without any SDK on either side. This page is the address book. Two step-by-step spokes: Reaper and TouchDesigner; the light half has its own page on Art-Net and sACN.

One rule before any mapping: silence means "not measured"

Every bio address is sent only while its own channel is actually measured. A finger that slips off the camera does not send a zero — it sends nothing, and your receiver holds its last value. That is deliberate: a structural 0 would collapse a bound scale or pull a grand master to black with no way to tell it from a performer who really stopped. Two consequences: a receiver that creates channels on first arrival (TouchDesigner's OSC In CHOP) will not show an address that has never been sent, and /echoelmusic/bio/coherence is the last to appear on every take because it needs sixteen accepted beats.

OSC out — UDP, default port 8000

Set the receiver's IP and port in Routing ▸ OSC and connect Body → OSC out in the patchbay. One datagram per message, no bundles. Floats throughout.

AddressRangeMeaning
/echoelmusic/bio/synthetic0 or 1Whose body this is: 1 = the built-in demo generator, 0 = a measured source. Sent first in every tick that carries a value; latch it as state.
/echoelmusic/bio/heart/bpm40–200Heart rate — only while a pulse is measured.
/echoelmusic/bio/heart/hrv0–1Normalised HRV — needs a pulse and a non-zero HRV.
/echoelmusic/bio/heart/rmssdmsRMSSD — only when the source can state it: strap and camera both refuse to state an HRV when fewer than 80 % of the raw beats survive hygiene.
/echoelmusic/bio/heart/sdnnmsSDNN — same rule.
/echoelmusic/bio/heart/pnn500–1pNN50 — same rule.
/echoelmusic/bio/breath/rate3–40Breaths per minute — sent only inside that plausibility band.
/echoelmusic/bio/breath/phase0–1Inhale→exhale phase, smooth — rides the rate's gate because 0 is a real position.
/echoelmusic/bio/coherence0–1HRV coherence — needs 16 accepted beats; on the camera about 16 seconds at rest, on a strap about 16 beats.
/echoelmusic/bio/event/heartbeatbangOne message per detected beat: [confidence, aux].
/echoelmusic/bio/event/breath/inhalebangInhale onset.

Three addresses exist in the sender and are not on the wire: /echoelmusic/bio/motion and /echoelmusic/bio/event/motion — never sent, nothing measures motion — and /echoelmusic/bio/event/eeg — never sent, no producer. Do not wait on them.

ADM-OSC — spatial objects, default port 9000

Connect Music or Body → ADM-OSC. Echoel drives one object, /adm/obj/{n}/… with n set in Routing, in the address shape of the ADM-OSC v1.0 spec that L-ISA, d&b Soundscape, Fletcher Machine and the open-source renderers read:

AddressRangeDriven by
/adm/obj/n/azim−180 … 180°pitch while notes sound; breath phase otherwise
/adm/obj/n/elev−90 … 90°pitch height while notes sound; HRV otherwise
/adm/obj/n/dist0 … 1level while notes sound; coherence otherwise (pulled close when coherent)
/adm/obj/n/gain0 … 1the master level while notes sound; not sent by the body arm

The body arm follows the same measured-only rule: an axis simply stops being driven rather than being sent to an extreme. Start your scene from a sane object position — Echoel will not push it to a default.

Light — Art-Net (UDP 6454) and sACN (UDP 5568)

One fixture's worth of DMX, unicast to the node or console IP you enter, universe selectable, resolution selectable. The channel map is the same for both protocols:

8-bit16-bit (coarse, fine)ChannelSource
11, 2Dimmerthe music's level; from the body alone, 0.3 + 0.7 × coherence (never fully dark)
23, 4Redthe sounding chord, transposed into visible light (CIE 1931, mixed in OKLab)
35, 6Green
47, 8Blue

Grand Master and Blackout sit in the app; the dimmer is slew-limited in the sender so a heartbeat can never strobe past 3 Hz. sACN packets carry the source name Echoelmusic (Echoelmusic (DEMO) on the demo body) so a console shows who is sending; a fixed priority of 100. Patch the desk's own fixtures on top — Echoel is a source, not a console. The long version: Art-Net and sACN from a phone.

MIDI out — a virtual source called "Echoelmusic"

Any DAW or synth on the device sees a CoreMIDI source named Echoelmusic the moment Music → MIDI out is routed. Notes go out on channel 1 by default; switch on MPE note layout in Routing to spread notes over member channels, and Per-note expression to send the body's glide, slide and press with each note (bend, CC74, channel pressure, stated immediately before the note-on). Over the network the same source is reachable through Apple's Network MIDI session, over USB through IDAM, over Bluetooth through the pairing picker in Routing. Clock: 24 PPQN MIDI clock with start/stop when the transport runs.

Receivers, in one line each

  • Reaper — OSC learn on any parameter or action; step by step.
  • TouchDesigner — OSC In CHOP on port 8000, one channel per address; step by step.
  • Resolume Arena / Avenue — Preferences ▸ OSC ▸ enable input on the port you set in Echoel; right-click any parameter ▸ Edit OSC, breathe, and it learns the address.
  • grandMA3, QLC+, any console — receive Art-Net or sACN on the universe you chose; patch a 4- or 8-channel generic RGB fixture with dimmer at the map above.
  • L-ISA, Soundscape, Fletcher Machine — point ADM-OSC at the renderer's OSC input; object index n in Routing.

What is not here

OSC control input — the one thing Echoel accepts

Switch on Routing ▸ OSC input ▸ Accept OSC control (off by default) and Echoel opens one UDP port (8001 by default, sender IPs restrictable) for a short whitelist of cues. Anything else is dropped before it reaches the instrument — no bio value comes in over the network, there is no play/stop cue, and there is no bidirectional session: the body goes out one way as above.

AddressArgumentEffect
/echoelmusic/ctrl/bpmfloat or intSets the locked tempo — only while the BPM is locked; in Flow the body owns the tempo and the cue is ignored. Logged as tempoSource=remoteControl.
/echoelmusic/ctrl/keyint 0–11Root pitch class (0 = C); the take retunes and recomposes like a header tap.
/echoelmusic/ctrl/scalestringA scale name (major, minor, dorian, …).
/echoelmusic/ctrl/genrestringA genre name as the app spells it (techHouse, jazz, …).
/echoelmusic/ctrl/visualStyleint 0–9The visual look.
/echoelmusic/ctrl/blackout0/1 or T/FArt-Net and sACN blackout together.

Motion and EEG addresses exist in the sender and are never sent. Live streaming was never built. Everything above is in the shipping app; nothing on this page is a roadmap item.

Integrating Echoel into a rig?

Tell us what the receiving end is — software and version, protocol, port, universe — and what you see. That is usually the whole answer.
