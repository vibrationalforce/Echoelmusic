## 1. Apple Pitches

### 1.1 Developer Relations Pitch

**Subject:** Inquiry: Echoelmusic – Bio-Reactive Music Instrument (HealthKit, BLE, MIDI, Art-Net, sACN, MPE) – Guidance on MFi & Developer Resources

Dear Apple Developer Relations Team,

My name is Michael Terbuyken. I am the developer of **Echoelmusic**, an open-source, on-device bio-reactive music instrument for iPhone and iPad, currently available via TestFlight.

Echoelmusic turns real-time physiological signals — heart rate and breathing — into musical expression. It uses HealthKit, camera-based rPPG, and BLE heart-rate sensors. Outputs include MIDI (with MPE support), OSC, Art-Net and sACN for lighting and spatial control. The app runs entirely on-device with a privacy-first architecture and is licensed under MIT.

A central aim is to make music creation more inclusive, so that people with different physical abilities can play and create through their body rather than only through traditional interfaces.

The longer-term direction is an inclusive multidimensional music and multimedia workstation. Depending on technical progress, this may include optional certified accessories that complement the iPhone experience.

I am reaching out to ask for guidance on the most appropriate path at this stage:

- Whether the **MFi program** is relevant for the kind of accessory we are considering
- Whether there are suitable Developer Relations or technical resources for deeper HealthKit and audio integration
- Any recommended next steps for an independent open-source project of this type

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Kind regards,  
Michael Terbuyken  
Echoel | Echoelmusic  
GitHub: https://github.com/vibrationalforce/Echoelmusic  
Website: https://echoelmusic.com

### 1.2 MFi Program Pitch

Dear MFi Program Team,

My name is Michael Terbuyken. I am the developer of **Echoelmusic**, an open-source, on-device bio-reactive music instrument for iPhone and iPad, currently in TestFlight.

Echoelmusic uses HealthKit, camera-based rPPG, and BLE heart-rate sensors to turn physiological signals into real-time musical expression. The app outputs via MIDI (including MPE), OSC, Art-Net and sACN. It is privacy-first, runs entirely on-device, and is licensed under MIT.
