# Generative Audio for Music Production (2024–2026): Systems, Real-Time/On-Device Fit, Licensing and Law — for Echoelmusic

Research date: 2026-10-16. Method: web search + direct reads of LICENSE/README files on raw.githubusercontent.com. Hugging Face, magenta.tensorflow.org and twobirds.com did NOT resolve from this sandbox (DNS failure), so model cards were not read directly; where a claim rests on secondary coverage it is marked. Status labels: **SHIPPED** (commercial service) · **OPEN** (usable open weights) · **RESEARCH**.

## 1. Commercial services — capabilities, APIs, terms, pricing, output rights

### Takeaway
The commercial field split in late 2025 into "licensed" (ElevenLabs Music, Stable Audio 2.5, Warner-settled Suno, UMG/Warner-settled Udio) and "still litigated" (Suno vs. UMG + Sony, with a second suit filed 2026-09-18). Every service is cloud-only, offline text/prompt-to-music; none is a real-time instrument except Google's Lyria RealTime (cloud streaming). Output rights are plan-gated and increasingly download-capped.

### Cited Findings
- **Suno (SHIPPED):** Warner Music settled with Suno in Nov 2025 (dated 25 or 26 Nov depending on report); Suno agreed to build new models trained only on licensed Warner music, replacing current models in 2026; Warner artists can opt in to voice/likeness use; downloads for paid users become capped — [Dubspot](https://blog.dubspot.com/suno-udio-ai-music-lawsuits-2026); [Rolling Stone AU](https://au.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/suno-warner-music-group-ai-music-settlement-lawsuit-87464)
- **Suno download caps from 2026-09-03:** Free = 7 lifetime downloads, personal use only; Pro = 20/month with commercial rights; Premier = 60/month; Studio = unlimited; playback/sharing unaffected — [Suno Help Center](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13614785); [Suno blog](https://suno.com/blog/suno-updates-tos). Retroactive application to existing libraries reported by [Mubert](https://mubert.com/blog/suno-download-caps) and [Dupple](https://dupple.com/learn/is-suno-free) (not confirmed on Suno's own page). A weaker source claims Suno owns free-tier output — [terms.law](https://terms.law/ai-output-rights/suno/) — conflicting; verify in current ToS.
- **Suno litigation still open:** UMG + Sony continue in D. Mass.; on 2026-08-18 Judge F. Dennis Saylor IV refused to add ~61,026 further recordings (wants the case decided on fair use) but allowed a DMCA §1201(a) anti-circumvention claim; labels filed a second suit 2026-09-18 in Boston targeting Suno v6 as "model laundering" (~60,202 recordings) — [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/universal-and-sony-sue-suno-for-a-second-time-claiming-platforms-v6-models-are-the-fruit-of-the-same-poisoned-tree/); [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/pro/umg-sony-hit-suno-with-lawsuit-after-new-ai-music-model/); [Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/music/music-industry-news/umg-sony-music-file-second-lawsuit-against-suno-1236705369/). UMG/Sony–Suno settlement talks reportedly hit an impasse in April 2026 — [OpusClip blog](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ai-music-news-april-2026) (secondary). Dispositive motions reportedly due 2027-04-09 — [chartlex tracker](https://www.chartlex.com/blog/business/music-industry-ai-lawsuits-tracker-2026) (secondary).
- **Udio (SHIPPED, in transition):** UMG settled late Oct 2025 (29 or 31 Oct); joint licensed platform planned for 2026, trained on authorised music; reported as a "walled garden" — [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/10/29/umg-udio-deal-everything-we-know/). Warner also settled with Udio in Nov 2025 — [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/warner-music-settles-udio-lawsuit-strikes-licensing-deal-with-ai-platform). Reports that tracks cannot be exported as MP3/WAV/stems or uploaded to DSPs, and that rollout is staged — [Sonilo](https://sonilo.com/blog/comparisons/udio-v4-warner-music-licensing-explained) (secondary, unconfirmed).
- **Settlement fallout:** the American Federation of Musicians sued UMG and Warner in NY federal court, arguing the settlements trigger payments to session musicians whose recordings were used — [Time To House](https://timetohouse.com/en/articles/musicians-union-sues-warner-universal-ai-suno-udio) (secondary).
- **ElevenLabs Eleven Music (SHIPPED, Aug 2025):** training data licensed via Merlin (indie labels) and Kobalt (publisher; artists opt in); marketed as cleared for commercial use; API available, 3 s–10 min durations — [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/news/2025/8/elevenlabs-launches-ai-music-platform-with-artist-backed-licensing-and-commercial-clearance); [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/eleven-music-review/). Pricing conflicting: ~$0.50/min (older) vs. up to 50 % API cuts with Music v2 (May 2026) — [invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/elevenlabs-music-ai-generator/). Self-serve plans reportedly exclude film/TV/games (enterprise covers all) — secondary, conflicting.
- **Stability Stable Audio 2.5 (SHIPPED, Sept 2025):** enterprise model, tracks up to 3 min, <2 s on GPU, "commercially safe and trained on a fully licensed dataset" — [Stability AI](https://stability.ai/news/stability-ai-introduces-stable-audio-25-the-first-audio-model-built-for-enterprise-sound-production-at-scale). Licensing deal with AudioSparx; partnerships with UMG and Warner on pro tools announced late 2025; Stable Audio 3 paper (May 2026) on licensed + CC data — [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/stability-ai-pushes-deeper-into-music-as-ai-audio-races-toward-licensing/); [AI Business](https://aibusiness.com/generative-ai/stability-ai-releases-audio-generation-model-for-enterprises).
- **Google Lyria (SHIPPED, API):** Gemini API lists Lyria 3.5 full song at $0.08/song; Lyria 3 previews now legacy; no free tier for Lyria music models — [Gemini API pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing); [benchlm](https://benchlm.ai/media-pricing/lyria). Outputs carry inaudible SynthID watermark; filters block artist-voice and copyrighted-lyric prompts — [invideo Lyria guide](https://invideo.io/blog/lyria-ai-music-generator/) (secondary). Lyria RealTime (streaming, the model behind MusicFX DJ) pricing was not found.
- **Riffusion → Producer.ai:** rebranded July 2025 (FUZZ-2.0 model, conversational UI); listed as acquired by Google Feb 2026 and reportedly now running on Lyria 3 under Google Labs — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/riffusion); [fast.io review](https://fast.io/resources/producer-ai-review-2026/) (secondary).
- **Meta AudioCraft/MusicGen:** code is open, but the **weights are CC BY-NC 4.0** (non-commercial) — [audiocraft LICENSE_weights](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/facebookresearch/audiocraft/main/LICENSE_weights).

### Inferences
- For an indie iOS app, the only commercially "clean" cloud APIs are those claiming licensed training data (ElevenLabs Music, Stable Audio 2.5, Google Lyria — the latter with SynthID). Suno/Udio have no public developer API usable for embedding in a third-party app, and their outputs are plan-gated; building on them would inherit their litigation risk and contradict "brand-honest".
- Any cloud call is a network dependency and a **zero-dependency / on-device** break for Echoelmusic, plus an App Store 5.1.2(i) disclosure (see §3).

### Gaps
- Lyria RealTime API price, quotas and output terms; ElevenLabs current API ToS on embedding in third-party apps; whether Udio's licensed platform has launched publicly — no primary source found.

## 2. Open and real-time models — size, latency, licences, on-device evidence

### Takeaway
Two open families are genuinely usable for a commercial instrument: **Magenta RealTime (v1 800M; v2 230M/2.4B)** — Apache 2.0 code + CC BY 4.0 weights, real-time but only demonstrated on TPU (v1) and Apple-Silicon Macs (v2, incl. an AUv3) — and **Stable Audio Open Small (341M)**, the only one with a demonstrated phone run (Arm CPU, ~11 s audio in <8 s; offline, not streaming). RAVE is the right *shape* (real-time, small, timbre) but its code is CC BY-NC 4.0. DDSP is Apache 2.0 and is already the conceptual basis of Echoelmusic's in-house `EchoelDDSP`. ACE-Step and YuE are Apache 2.0 but GPU-class, offline, and undisclosed in training data.

### Cited Findings
- **Magenta RealTime v1 (OPEN, June 2025):** 800M-param autoregressive transformer, trained on ~190k hours of stock music from multiple sources, mostly instrumental; real-time factor 1.6 on free Colab TPU v2-8 (2 s audio in 1.25 s) — [Magenta RT page](https://magenta.tensorflow.org/magenta-realtime); [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jun/20/magenta-realtime); tech report [arXiv 2508.04651](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.04651v1). Licence: code Apache 2.0, weights CC BY 4.0, plus usage terms "do not generate content … that infringe … rights", and "Google claims no rights in outputs you generate"; users solely responsible for outputs — [v1_legacy README](https://github.com/magenta/magenta-realtime/tree/v1_legacy) (read directly).
- **Magenta RealTime 2 (OPEN, ~June 2026):** components MusicCoCa (text/audio style embedding), SpectroStream (codec), Depthformer (token generator); sizes `mrt2_small` 230M (real-time on any Apple-Silicon Mac incl. M1 Air) and `mrt2_base` 2.4B (needs M2 Pro-Max class); C++ engine `magentart::core` on MLX; ships example **AUv3 plugin** and standalone macOS app; JAX/MLX Python library; offline inference also on NVIDIA — [magenta-realtime README](https://github.com/magenta/magenta-realtime) and [docs/models.md](https://github.com/magenta/magenta-realtime/blob/main/docs/models.md) (read directly). Repo LICENSE = Apache 2.0 (read directly). Weights CC BY 4.0, ~200 ms end-to-end control latency, 40 ms frames, MIDI note control — [mer.vin](https://mer.vin/2026/06/magenta-realtime-2-open-local-live-music-model-with-midi-and-200ms-latency/); [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/4258/magenta-realtime-2-google-open-ai-music-model) (secondary; the MRT2 README on `main` carries no licence-terms section, so the weight licence was not confirmed from primary text).
- **Stable Audio Open Small (OPEN, May 2025):** 341M params, built with Arm, runs on smartphone Arm CPUs, up to 11 s audio generated in <8 s — [Stability AI](https://stability.ai/news/stability-ai-and-arm-release-stable-audio-open-small-enabling-real-world-deployment-for-on-device-audio-control). Licence: Stability Community License — Stability says free for commercial and non-commercial use; Gigazine reports a commercial revenue cap of US$1M/yr — [Gigazine](https://www.gigazine.net/news/20250515-stable-audio-open-small/) (conflict; the Community License is generally understood to carry the $1M threshold — verify on the model card). Inference code `stable-audio-tools` is MIT — [LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Stability-AI/stable-audio-tools/main/LICENSE) (read directly).
- **RAVE (IRCAM, OPEN code / RESEARCH-grade):** VAE generating 48 kHz audio, 20× faster than real-time on a laptop CPU — [arXiv 2111.05011](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.05011); streaming via cached/non-causal convolutions, shipped as Max/Pd `nn~` externals and a VST — [arXiv 2204.07064](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.07064); [IRCAM forum](https://forum.ircam.fr/article/rave-model-challenge). **Code licence: CC BY-NC 4.0** — [acids-ircam/RAVE LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/acids-ircam/RAVE/master/LICENSE) (read directly); community checkpoints also typically CC BY-NC — [mirror](https://huggingface-gw-kz.just-ai.com/AEmotionStudio/rave-models) (secondary). No iOS/Core ML RAVE port found.
- **DDSP (Google Magenta, OPEN):** Apache 2.0, © 2019 Google — [magenta/ddsp LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/magenta/ddsp/main/LICENSE) (read directly).
- **ACE-Step v1 (OPEN, 3.5B):** Apache 2.0; diffusion + DCAE + linear transformer; up to 4 min of music in ~20 s on A100; RTF 27× (27 steps) on A100; LoRAs for vocals, rap, stems — [ACE-Step README](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step) (read directly). Training-data provenance not disclosed in README.
- **YuE (OPEN):** Apache 2.0 — [YuE LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE/main/LICENSE) (read directly). Full-song lyrics-to-song; GPU-class.
- **MusicGen (incl. small):** weights CC BY-NC 4.0 — [audiocraft](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/facebookresearch/audiocraft/main/LICENSE_weights).
- Closest Core ML precedent: a speech model (Sidon) ported to the iPhone Neural Engine, split into encoder/decoder packages, on-device compiled; ANE compiler failed at 600-frame encoder input — [HF sidon-coreml](https://huggingface.co/seanll95/sidon-coreml).

### Inferences
- **Licence matrix for a paid/commercial iOS app:** OK = Magenta RT v1/v2 (Apache + CC BY 4.0, attribution required), DDSP (Apache), ACE-Step/YuE (Apache code+weights — but unknown training data = provenance risk post-GEMA), Stable Audio Open Small (OK under $1M revenue, with attribution/licence terms). NOT OK without a separate licence = RAVE (code NC), MusicGen weights (NC).
- **On-phone real-time:** no source shows any generative *music* model streaming in real time on an iPhone. MRT2-small (230M) running real-time on an M1 Air is the nearest signal (A-series iPhones share architecture lineage, but no measurement exists). This is a research step, not a shippable claim.
- MRT2 additionally requires MLX + a C++ engine — this would break Echoelmusic's "Swift 100 %, zero dependencies" rule (C++ only for Council-approved free libs per CLAUDE.md).

### Gaps
- MRT2 weight-licence text and training data from primary source (HF unreachable). Stable Audio Open Small exact licence threshold text. Any measured iPhone latency for MRT2-small, RAVE or DDSP-autoencoder.
- MusicGen's training data is recalled (from the MusicGen paper, arXiv 2306.05284) as ~20k hours of licensed music (internal + Shutterstock/Pond5) — not re-fetched this session.

## 3. Legal landscape — US suits, settlements, Germany/EU, labelling, App Store

### Takeaway
Germany is now the hardest jurisdiction for training-on-unlicensed-music: Munich Regional Court held OpenAI (lyrics, Nov 2025) and Suno (compositions, 31 Jul 2026) liable, treating memorisation in weights as reproduction, limiting §44b TDM to data-prep copying, and even reaching US-based training. Both are first-instance and under appeal. EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency duties apply from 2 Aug 2026 (machine-readable marking of AI-generated audio; extended to 2 Dec 2026 for systems on the market before 2 Aug). Apple's guideline 5.1.2(i) requires explicit consent and provider disclosure before sending personal data to third-party AI.

### Cited Findings
- **GEMA v OpenAI (LG München I, 42 O 14139/24, 11 Nov 2025):** memorisation of lyrics in model and outputs = reproduction; §44b UrhG TDM covers creating training data but not memorising reproduction in training; damages €4,620.70, cease-and-desist, disclosure; personality-rights claim rejected; on appeal to OLG München — [Bird & Bird](https://cm.twobirds.com/en/insights/2025/landmark-ruling-of-the-munich-regional-court-(gema-v-openai)-on-copyright-and-ai-training); [Verfassungsblog](https://verfassungsblog.de/munich-regional-courts-ruling-in-gema-v-open-ai/); [William Fry](https://www.williamfry.com/knowledge/gema-v-openai-europes-direction-on-ai-infringement/).
- **GEMA v Suno (LG München I, 42 O 763/25, 31 Jul 2026, not final):** prohibited for six compositions: reproduction for training in the US, memorisation in models on German servers, making the model available, and reproduction/communication via outputs; jurisdiction over US training via §131 VGG; applied US law and rejected fair use; Suno stream-ripped from YouTube circumventing "Rolling Cipher"; disclosure + damages; Suno weighing appeal — [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/suno-loses-ai-lawsuit-gema-1236825010/); [Bristows](https://inquisitiveminds.bristows.com/post/102ngsl/gema-v-suno-munich-court-finds-ai-music-training-and-outputs-infringe-copyright); [JUVE Patent](https://www.juve-patent.com/cases/munich-regional-court-stops-suno-using-gema-protected-music/); [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/07/ai-copyright-lawsuit-germany-suno-1237014566/).
- **US:** RIAA-backed UMG/Sony v. Suno pending (fair use the core issue; §1201 claim added); second suit vs. v6 — see §1 citations. UMG and Warner settled with Udio; Warner with Suno — see §1.
- **EU AI Act Art. 50:** obligations in force 2 Aug 2026; Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content published June 2026, judged adequate by Commission (8 Jul) and AI Board (9 Jul) for Art. 50(2), (4), (5); final Commission guidelines 20 Jul 2026 — [Paul Weiss](https://www.paulweiss.com/insights/client-memos/eu-finalises-transparency-rules-for-ai-generated-content); [Reed Smith](https://www.reedsmith.com/our-insights/blogs/viewpoints/102nbz0/transparency-obligations-for-ai-generated-content-the-code-of-practice-adequacy/); [Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2026/08/EU-AI-Act-Transparency-Obligations-for-AI-Generated-Content-Article-50); [EC FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/signing-code-practice-transparency-ai-generated-content). Provider marking (50(2)) covers audio; baseline described as signed metadata + robust watermark; providers asked to offer a (free) detection mechanism; narrow exclusions reportedly include ephemeral real-time content; 2 Dec 2026 extended deadline for systems already on the market — [aiethicsassessor](https://aiethicsassessor.com/blog/how-to-label-ai-generated-content-in-the-eu); [artificialintelligenceact.eu](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/transparency-rules-article-50/) (commentary; Code's audio-specific technical text not read).
- **Watermarking in practice:** Google Lyria outputs carry SynthID — [invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/lyria-ai-music-generator/) (secondary). No source found specifying a C2PA audio profile requirement.
- **Apple App Store 5.1.2(i)** (updated 13 Nov 2025), current text: "You must clearly disclose where personal data will be shared with third parties, including with third-party AI, and obtain explicit permission before doing so." — [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) (read directly); [Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/news/apple-app-review-guidelines-on-ai). No separate App Store rule requiring labels on AI-generated *content* was found.

### Inferences
- Under GEMA v Suno, a German court can reach a model *offered* in Germany regardless of where it was trained. An indie app that bundles or proxies a model trained on unlicensed music (or undisclosed data — ACE-Step, YuE) carries "communication to the public via the model" exposure; a model with disclosed licensed/stock/CC data (Magenta RT stock music, Stable Audio Open on Freesound/FMA-style CC data, Stable Audio 2.5 licensed) is materially safer.
- Art. 50(2) attaches to the *provider* of the generative system. If Echoelmusic itself ships a model that outputs synthetic audio, it is plausibly a provider and must mark output machine-readably (metadata in exported files + watermark), unless the "ephemeral real-time" exclusion covers live-only sound (reported, not verified in primary text). Exports (`SingleExport`, `RetroCapture`) would not be ephemeral.
- Sending bio data (HR, HRV — health data) to any cloud AI (Lyria, ElevenLabs) triggers 5.1.2(i) consent + provider naming, and conflicts with Echoel's `BioEgressPolicy` posture; on-device inference avoids it.
- Brand-honesty: per CLAUDE.md, store/website copy may claim only what ships; any "AI music" claim invites the same scrutiny as the labels' suits. Calling a DDSP/timbre model "generative AI" is accurate only if a learned model actually ships.

### Gaps
- Status of OpenAI's appeal at OLG München and whether Suno appealed; exact Code-of-Practice wording on audio and on real-time/ephemeral output; whether a small on-device instrument model counts as a "general-purpose" or "generative AI system" provider under Art. 50 — no primary text read.

## 4. Fit for a "the body drives the sound" instrument — real-time timbre vs. offline text-to-music

### Takeaway
Offline text-to-music (Suno, Udio, ElevenLabs, Lyria song, ACE-Step, YuE, Stable Audio Open) produces finished clips from prompts — the opposite of an instrument where heart and breath modulate sound continuously at ~1 Hz. The architectural fit is (a) differentiable/real-time timbre synthesis (DDSP — already Echoel's in-house path; RAVE-style latent decoders) and (b) streaming control-conditioned generators (Magenta RT 2, Lyria RealTime), with bio signals mapped to style embeddings / note controls.

### Cited Findings
- RAVE streams in buffer-based real time with cached convolutions and runs 20–80× real-time on laptop CPU — [arXiv 2204.07064](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.07064); [arXiv 2111.05011](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.05011).
- MRT2 is built for live control: style embeddings from text/audio (MusicCoCa), note control app (`examples/jam`), prompt-space explorer (`examples/collider`), AUv3 plugin — [magenta-realtime README](https://github.com/magenta/magenta-realtime).
- Stable Audio Open Small is explicitly positioned for "on-device audio control" but generates ≤11 s clips in batch — [Stability AI](https://stability.ai/news/stability-ai-and-arm-release-stable-audio-open-small-enabling-real-world-deployment-for-on-device-audio-control).
- ACE-Step's speed (RTF 27× on A100) is datacenter-GPU; no mobile figure — [ACE-Step README](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step).

### Inferences
- **Recommended ladder for Echoelmusic (inference, not measured):**
  1. **Now / zero-risk:** keep in-house Swift `EchoelDDSP` (DDSP is Apache 2.0, no training data, no Art. 50 provider question because no learned generator) — the brand-honest "bio-reactive synthesis".
  2. **Next research step:** a small learned timbre decoder (DDSP-autoencoder or RAVE-style) trained by Echoel on **its own or CC0/licensed** material, exported to Core ML. RAVE's *code* is NC, so either licence it from IRCAM or reimplement from the paper; training data must be clean (post-GEMA).
  3. **Mac/AUv3 or future high-end:** Magenta RT 2 small (230M, Apache + CC BY 4.0) as a bio-steered streaming layer — bio maps to style-embedding interpolation and note density; requires MLX/C++ (Council decision against zero-dep rule) and an iPhone latency measurement that does not yet exist. ~200 ms control latency is acceptable because bio applies at ~1 Hz.
  4. **Avoid:** Suno/Udio (no embeddable API, litigation), MusicGen/RAVE checkpoints (NC), unknown-provenance weights for anything shipped in the EU.
- If any learned generator ships and its audio can be exported, plan Art. 50 marking (metadata + watermark) and an in-app/user-facing disclosure from day one; keep bio data on-device to avoid 5.1.2(i).

### Gaps
- No measurement exists of any of these models' real-time performance on an A17/A18/A19 iPhone; no public Core ML conversion of RAVE or MRT2 found.
